The rules

Use these in your worksheet and homework.

Two questions, every time

IS IT LEGAL? Were you allowed — by the owner, by the law?
IS IT RIGHT? Is it fair and honest? Who could get hurt?

Found a flaw?
STOP Go no further. Do not test it, copy it or share it.
TELL Tell the owner privately. Say what you saw.
WAIT Give them time to fix it. Tell nobody else first.
The law
LawWhat it says
Qatar
Law No. 14 of 2014, Article 3		 A crime to: get into a system without the right · go beyond what you are allowed · stay in once you know you should not. Up to 3 years in prison, a fine up to QR 500,000, or both. Doubled if data is changed, deleted or shared.
UK
Computer Misuse Act 1990		 Here to compare. Unauthorised access: up to 2 years.

The line is permission. Neither law asks WHY you did it. Both ask: were you ALLOWED?
